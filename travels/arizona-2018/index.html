Arizona Adventure

March 2018

In March 2018 we headed to Arizona.

Days covered Tombstone’s streets and stagecoaches, the O.K. Corral, King Solomon Territorial Masonic Lodge No. 5, Tombstone Brewing Company, a stop under the Sierra Vista welcome sign, and a sunset on the tarmac heading home.
