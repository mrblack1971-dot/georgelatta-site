Edisto & Yorktown

June 10, 2018

On June 10, 2018 Grandpa, Jessica, Jasmine, and I took a camper trip to the coast.

We toured USS Yorktown at Patriots Point — the flight deck, galley, and bridge — then played in the surf at Edisto Beach.

One lawn frame includes a friend playing with Jessica and Jasmine in front of Yorktown — captioned that way.
