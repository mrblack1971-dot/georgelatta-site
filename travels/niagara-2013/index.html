Niagara Falls

4th of July (c. 2013)

Over a 4th of July we drove north to Niagara Falls and to visit family.

The album is undated on Facebook; photo IDs place it after our March 2013 North Carolina road trip and before Myrtle Beach April 2014, so the Travel Journal uses July 2013 as a placement date.

Jessica and Jasmine rode along in pajamas past the Pennsylvania and West Virginia welcome signs, then walked the boardwalk above the mist.

Some frames from the backyard 4th of July gathering include friends’ kids at the party — kept here with captions that say so.
