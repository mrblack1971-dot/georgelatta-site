Florida family trip

October 2021

In October 2021 we rented a house and spent time at Universal Orlando with Jessica, Jasmine, and family.

The days covered Halloween Horror Nights, the Wizarding World (Knight Bus, Hogwarts Express, Three Broomsticks), and Springfield’s Moe’s Tavern — a great time with family.
