Nevada

2021

In 2021 we took a family trip through Nevada.

The route covered the Extraterrestrial Highway, Hoover Dam, Fremont Street, Paris Las Vegas, the Bellagio, the Forum Shops, and the Venetian canals — with Jessica, Jasmine, my redhead companion, and family along for the ride.
