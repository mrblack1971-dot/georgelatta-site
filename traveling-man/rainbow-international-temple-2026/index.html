International Temple, Supreme Assembly, Order of the Rainbow for Girls

August 1, 2026 · McAlester, Oklahoma

Photo coming soon

On the drive home from the Supreme Session in Tucson, Arizona (held at El Conquistador Tucson, A Hilton Resort), we stopped in McAlester to visit the International Temple, Supreme Assembly, Order of the Rainbow for Girls.

The Order of the Rainbow for Girls was founded in McAlester on April 6, 1922, by Rev. W. Mark Sexson. The temple was built in 1950–1951 as the Order’s international headquarters and was listed on the National Register of Historic Places in 2013.
